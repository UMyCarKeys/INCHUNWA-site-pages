bio: "Lisa Fruichantie is an activist, futurist, entrepreneur, designer, and thought leader whose work centers on cultivating transformative shared spaces and building bridges across communities. A member of the Seminole Nation of Oklahoma, she was born in the Ute hunting territories of Southern Colorado and raised between winters with the Kenaitze people in Alaska, where she was immersed in Dena'ina culture, and summers in Oklahoma and the Four Corners region, where she reconnected with her Southeastern Tribal culture through her grandparents. Now based on the traditional lands of the Puyallup people in Tacoma, Washington, Fruichantie brings these lived connections into work that honors the sovereignty of art, culture, and heritage. Through activism, creative expression, communal interaction, and the vision of the Inchunwa Project, she advances initiatives that support positive social change, rematriation, and multigenerational impact for collective healing."
