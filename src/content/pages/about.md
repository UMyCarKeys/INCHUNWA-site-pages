## Our Mission

Inchunwa is a community-rooted Southeastern Tribal project and podcast that promotes body sovereignty through Indigenous Mississippian identity, cultural ancestral marking practices, collective learning, and knowledge-sharing across in-person and digital platforms.

**Founded: 2018**

## Our Vision

Inchunwa's vision for the future is grounded in education. We strive to be a trusted resource for information on ancestral markings and designs, the historical context of Indigenous tattooing practices, and how these traditions are evolving today. Our educational efforts extend beyond online content and include direct work with communities, collaboration with tattoo practitioners, and relationship-building with cultural knowledge keepers. Through this work, we support the reclamation, protection, and continuation of Southeastern Indigenous tattoo traditions for future generations.

## What Sets Us Apart

Our organization was founded by tribal community members to revivalize a cultural practice that has been asleep and, in some cases, forced underground for over 100 years across many Southeastern Indigenous communities. While the revivalization and stewardship of cultural practices have often been held within tribal governments, this project is led by community members and intentionally invites broader community participation in the work of restoration.

Globally, Indigenous tattoo revivalization efforts frequently center tattoo practitioners as the primary drivers and knowledge holders of the movement. Our organization is unique in that we are building a model where community members are not only recipients of ancestral markings, but active participants in sustaining the cultural, relational, and ceremonial foundations of the practice. In this way, community involvement is not peripheral, it is intrinsic to the work of reclaiming ancestral marks.

## Who We Serve

The Inchunwa Project serves southeastern moundbuilder descendant people as well as researchers, academics, grant makers, media organizations, and anyone seeking to understand and support the revivalization of Indigenous tattooing traditions.
